## Apple Music-like Lyrics

Apple Music-like Lyrics (AMLL) is an AGPL-3.0-only project used during LyricStage design and technical evaluation as a quality benchmark for timed-word rendering, line layout, spring motion, seek behavior and accessibility fallbacks.

The current LyricStage production bundle does not include @applemusic-like-lyrics/core, @applemusic-like-lyrics/react or @applemusic-like-lyrics/ttml, and no AMLL release-file source was copied into the renderer. If future work copies, modifies or links AMLL code, this notice must be expanded with the upstream copyright, exact modified files, modification statement and corresponding-source location.

Upstream project: https://github.com/Steve-xmh/applemusic-like-lyrics
